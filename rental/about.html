Who We Are

A New Company With Deep Roots

Hometown Rentals of Central Florida LLC was officially established on January 5th, 2026 — but the experience behind it goes back over four decades.

Our founder spent 40+ years in the trenches of property management throughout Highlands County, learning every facet of the business: what makes a great tenant, how to keep properties profitable, what owners really need, and how to build lasting trust with the communities we serve.

When we launched Hometown Rentals, we brought all of that knowledge together with a fresh vision: a modern, client-first property management firm that feels genuinely local — because it is. We're not a franchise. We're not a call center. We're your neighbors, and we take that seriously.
