A College Woven Into the Community It Serves

Founded in 2015 as the Bishop James Taite Bible Institute, Gateway Global Bible College & Seminary grew out of decades of local outreach ministry in Brewton. Today, under the leadership of Chief Apostle Dr. Shirley Caesar Taite, the college trains students from across South Alabama and the Florida Panhandle — with an outreach network already feeding and supporting hundreds of local families each year.

Whether you're a rural church planter, a youth leader in a small congregation, or a lay leader ready to step into your calling, Gateway Global was built with your context in mind.
