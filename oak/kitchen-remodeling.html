Kitchen questions

What homeowners ask us most.

How long will my kitchen remodel take?

Most kitchens take 6–10 weeks from demolition to reveal. Refresh-tier projects can complete in 4–6 weeks. Estate-tier projects with structural changes can run 12–16 weeks. You'll receive a written schedule on day one.

Will I be able to live in my home during the remodel?

Yes — most clients do. We set up a temporary kitchen space, install dust barriers daily, and clean the work zone before leaving each evening.

Quartz, granite, or marble — what should I pick?

Quartz is the workhorse for South Florida kitchens — engineered, non-porous, low maintenance. Granite reads more traditional and is fully natural. Marble is breathtaking but requires care. We'll bring samples to your home.

Do you handle Palm Beach County permits?

Always. We pull all permits, schedule inspections, and close them out. You never need to call the building department.

What's your warranty?

5 years on workmanship, in writing, transferable to the next homeowner. Manufacturer warranties on appliances and materials are passed through to you in full.
