Common questions

What homeowners ask us first.

How much does a kitchen remodel cost in West Palm Beach?

Most full kitchen remodels in Palm Beach County run $48K–$120K, with luxury projects reaching $180K+. Bathrooms typically run $18K–$55K. Your final number depends on cabinetry, countertops, and any layout changes — we'll give you a fixed written estimate before any contract is signed.

How long will my project take?

A primary bath takes 3–6 weeks. A full kitchen typically takes 6–10 weeks from demo to reveal. We provide a written schedule on day one and update you weekly.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Florida CBC1264118, fully insured for liability and workers' compensation, and NARI-certified for kitchen and bath remodeling. Documentation is available on request.

Do you handle permits?

Always. We pull all required Palm Beach County permits and manage inspections from start to finish — you never need to call the building department.
