How we work

A remodel without the surprises.

Six steps, one project manager, one written schedule, and weekly photo updates. Here's exactly what happens from the day you call us to the day we hand over the keys.

01

Discovery Visit · Week 1

What we do: A 60–90 minute walk-through of your space. We measure, photograph, and listen — to how you cook, bathe, store, host. No sales pitch, no pressure.

What we need from you: Inspiration photos, a rough budget range, and an honest list of what's not working today.

02

Design & Selections · Weeks 2–4

What we do: Floor plans, 3D renderings, and curated material boards (cabinetry, countertops, tile, plumbing, lighting). Two design revisions included.

What you get: A clear visual of the finished room before a single demo swing.

03

Fixed Written Proposal · Week 4

What we do: Line-item pricing for every cabinet, every countertop slab, every fixture. No allowances that balloon, no "to be determined."

What you get: A signed contract with a fixed total, a written schedule, and a payment plan tied to milestones — never up front.

04

Pre-Construction · Weeks 5–8

What we do: Order long-lead items (cabinets, stone, custom glass), pull all Palm Beach County permits, schedule subs, and stage materials in our warehouse — so when demo starts, the whole project is in our hands.

What you get: A start date that doesn't move.

05

Build · 3–10 weeks on site

What we do: One dedicated project manager. One vetted crew (no rotating subs). Daily dust containment with HEPA filtration and floor protection from the door to the work zone. Weekly photo & progress reports every Friday.

What you get: A predictable rhythm, a clean home, and a single phone number to call.

06

Reveal & 5-Year Warranty · Final week

What we do: A walkthrough with you, a punch list closed within 5 business days, all manuals organized in a binder, and a written 5-year warranty handed over.

What you get: A finished room, a warranty you can hold in your hand, and our cell phone number for the next five years.

Process FAQ

What homeowners ask before signing.

How firm is your fixed price?

Firm. The only time the price changes is if you change the scope (e.g. "let's also redo the laundry while you're here"). All change orders are written, signed, and priced before any work is done.

What if you find something behind the walls?

We carry a 5% contingency line item in every estimate for hidden conditions (rot, old wiring, plumbing failures). If we don't use it, you don't pay it.

Can we live in the house during the remodel?

Almost always, yes — especially for bathrooms (we sequence so a guest bath stays usable). For kitchens, most clients set up a temporary kitchen in the dining room; we'll help you plan it.

How do payments work?

10% at contract signing, then milestone payments tied to completion (rough-in, cabinet install, final). Never more than 50% paid until the project is more than 50% done.
