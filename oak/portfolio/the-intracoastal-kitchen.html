The Intracoastal Kitchen

A 320-square-foot kitchen reimagined for a family of four who both cook — and entertain twenty at a time, three months a year.

Location

Palm Beach, FL

Scope

Full kitchen + butler's pantry

Duration

9 weeks on site

Investment

$140K–$160K range

The challenge

A galley that fought the view.

The original 1998 kitchen was a closed galley with the sink facing a wall — backwards to the Intracoastal view just six feet behind it. Two cooks couldn't pass without sidestepping, and the dining room felt severed from the room where everyone actually wanted to be.

The solution

One island, one wall removed, one view restored.

We pulled the load-bearing wall between kitchen and dining (with a flush steel beam concealed above), spun the prep sink toward the water, and built a 10-foot Calacatta-topped island with seating for five. A butler's pantry — built into what was a coat closet — handles the espresso machine, second dishwasher, and the bar.

Materials & finishes

  • Cabinetry: Custom white oak inset, hand-rubbed natural finish, soft-close throughout
  • Island countertop: Honed Calacatta Gold, single slab, mitered 3" edge
  • Perimeter countertops: Caesarstone Cloudburst Concrete quartz
  • Hardware: Unlacquered brass cup pulls (Rejuvenation Ballard)
  • Appliances: Wolf 48" dual-fuel range, Sub-Zero 48" refrigerator, two Miele dishwashers
  • Lighting: Visual Comfort Bistro pendants over island, Lutron RadioRA recessed
