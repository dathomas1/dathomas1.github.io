The Coastal Primary Bath

A primary suite bath rebuilt around a wet room — and finished entirely in materials that won't quit on you in 80% humidity.

Location

Jupiter, FL

Scope

Primary bath + walk-in closet

Duration

5 weeks on site

Investment

$52K–$60K range

The challenge

A 1990s bath, mildewing slowly.

Original drywall behind the shower, a stand-alone tub no one used, builder-grade vinyl plank, and a vanity wall losing its veneer to humidity. The clients wanted a hotel bath — but didn't want to fight South Florida moisture for the next twenty years.

The solution

A wet room. A real one.

We demolished the entire shower-tub corner and rebuilt it as a curbless wet room — a single waterproofed zone holding both the walk-in shower and a freestanding tub. Linear drain, full-height limestone walls, and a frameless glass divider that keeps the rest of the room dry without closing the space in.

Materials chosen for South Florida

  • Walls & floor: Honed Jura limestone, sealed twice — handles humidity without staining
  • Vanity: Marine-grade plywood box, rift-cut white oak doors, finished with conversion varnish (not lacquer)
  • Countertop: Quartz (Caesarstone Statuario Maximus) — zero porosity, no resealing
  • Tub: Victoria + Albert volcanic limestone freestanding
  • Plumbing: Brizo Litze, brushed brass — selected for its salt-air-tested PVD finish
  • Ventilation: Panasonic 110 CFM humidity-sensing fan on a 20-min timer
  • Floor heat: Schluter Ditra-Heat under the limestone (yes, even in Florida — mornings)
