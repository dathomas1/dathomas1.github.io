Bath questions

What homeowners ask us most.

How long does a bathroom remodel take?

Powder rooms typically complete in 2–3 weeks. Primary baths run 4–6 weeks. Spa-tier suites with structural changes can run 7–9 weeks.

Walk-in shower or keep the tub?

For most West Palm Beach primary baths, a walk-in shower increases resale value and daily use. We recommend keeping at least one tub in the home for resale to families with young children.

What tile holds up best in South Florida humidity?

Porcelain — full stop. Large-format porcelain with epoxy or urethane grout minimizes mold growth. We use Schluter waterproofing membranes on every shower pan.

Do I need to relocate during the project?

Almost never. If your bath is the only one, we work with you on scheduling so plumbing downtime is minimized.

Are permits required?

Yes — anything beyond a fixture swap requires Palm Beach County permits. We handle all of them.
