Local FAQ

Palm Beach specifics.

Do you handle ARCOM submittals?

Yes — we prepare and submit all required drawings, material samples, and color elevations directly to the Architectural Commission. Our average approval timeline is one cycle.

Can you work in our condo with strict construction hours?

Yes. We default to 8a–5p weekdays, coordinate with building management on freight, and provide certificates of insurance naming the association.

Will materials survive the salt air?

The right materials will. We specify PVD-finished plumbing, marine-grade substrates, and stainless or solid brass hardware — and we'll show you why on a sample board before you choose.

Do you work north of Royal Poinciana?

Yes — all of Palm Beach island, plus West Palm Beach, Palm Beach Gardens, and North Palm Beach.
