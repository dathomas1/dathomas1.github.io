Why local matters

What Jupiter homes need that Atlanta homes don't.

Salt-air-tested materials

Solid brass, PVD-finished, or 316 stainless hardware — never the chrome-plated builder-grade fixtures that pit out in three years east of US-1.

Humidity-tolerant cabinetry

Marine-grade plywood boxes, conversion-varnish finishes, vented toe-kicks. Particle-board cabinetry doesn't belong in a coastal bathroom — and we won't install it.

Hurricane-aware planning

Impact glass coordination, shutter clearance, and a plan for what happens to your half-finished kitchen if a storm enters the cone in August.

Local FAQ

Jupiter specifics.

Do you work in Jupiter Inlet Colony & Tequesta too?

Yes — all three municipalities, plus Juno Beach and North Palm Beach. Different permit offices, same crew.

What materials hold up east of US-1?

Solid brass or PVD-finished plumbing, 316 stainless or solid brass hardware, marine-grade plywood cabinet boxes, quartz countertops (zero porosity), and porcelain or natural stone floors. We'll show you specs on a sample board.

How do you handle hurricane season?

For projects active June–November, we stockpile critical materials in our climate-controlled warehouse, keep your exterior envelope buttoned up at end of each day, and have a written storm-prep protocol that triggers when a system enters the cone.

Do you build in Admirals Cove / Loxahatchee Club?

Yes — both communities, plus Trump National, Old Marsh, and the Bears Club. We're familiar with their architectural review processes.
