Your DSCR — Does This Property Pay For Itself?

Here's something most banks never tell you. There's a type of loan called a DSCR loan — Debt Service Coverage Ratio. Unlike a conventional loan it doesn't qualify you based on your personal income or tax returns. It qualifies you based on whether the property earns enough in rent to cover its own costs.

To calculate this we need to estimate your PITIA — Principal, Interest, Taxes, Insurance, and Associations. Lenders want to see that the rent covers this — ideally at a ratio of 1.0 or higher.
